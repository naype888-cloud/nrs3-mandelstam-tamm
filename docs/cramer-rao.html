How much of FQ can position read?

A phase θ is written by transport, e−iθT, and read with position P. The quantum Fisher information is FQ = 4 Var T; reading with P gives FP = |⟨[T, P]⟩|² / Var P. Lean: FP ≤ FQ, always. Turn the phase of the state and watch the two fluctuation vectors open and close.
